About Clip Games

Clip Games is an independent game studio focused on building social casual games. We believe great games don't need to be complicated — they need to be fun, accessible, and easy to share with friends.

Our team builds for platforms where people already spend their time: WeChat Mini Programs, mobile browsers, and social apps. No downloads, no friction — just tap and play.
